# Stage card pictures

The pictures on the stage selection cards. They illustrate the challenge; they are not
captures of the simulator and do not show the exact parts of the answer robots.

| File | Card |
|---|---|
| `raise-flag.jpg` | Stage 1, Raise the flag |
| `finish-line.jpg` | Stage 2, Cross the finish line |
| `wall-brake.jpg` | Stage 3, Brake before the wall |
| `lab.jpg` | Free building (Lab) |

## Provenance

- Generated on 2026-11-12 with the built-in image generation of the Codex CLI (0.159.0-alpha.9),
  one image per prompt, from text prompts only. No reference images were uploaded.
- Requested by the project owner on 2026-11-12 to replace the drawn stage diagrams.
- Each 1536x1024 PNG was centre-cropped to 16:9 (80 px off the top and bottom) and resized to
  960x540 JPEG, quality 88. The PNG originals and the prompt files are kept outside the repository.

## Shared style prompt

Clean 3D product render that looks like a studio photo of a small educational robot kit on a matte
light-gray tabletop; soft daylight from the upper left; neutral white-gray background; silver
anodized perforated beams and plates, black rubber wheels with yellow hubs, yellow TT gear motors,
one green circuit board, a blue micro servo; no text, letters, numbers, logos, people or hands.

## Scene prompts

- `raise-flag`: a robot arm of two perforated beams driven by a blue micro servo lifts an orange
  flag level with a deep green mark on a white pole.
- `finish-line`: a two-wheeled kit car crosses a checkered finish line under a red banner arch on a
  dark-gray road strip lined with orange cones.
- `wall-brake`: a two-wheeled kit car with an ultrasonic sensor on its front is stopped inside a
  yellow-bordered zone a short distance from a plain gray wall.
- `lab`: loose kit parts laid out in neat rows: beams, plates, wheels, gear motors, a servo, a
  circuit board, a four-cell battery holder, an ultrasonic sensor and bolts.
